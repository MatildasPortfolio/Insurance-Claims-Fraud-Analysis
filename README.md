# Insurance-Claims-Fraud-Analysis
## Overview
This project focuses on analysing automobile insurance claims data to identify patterns associated with potentially fraudulent claims. Using SQL and Power BI, the analysis explores fraud risk across incident types, severity levels, customer demographics, locations, vehicle makes, and time of day. The project aims to turn claims data into clear insights that can help an insurance company better understand fraud risk and make more informed decisions.

## Business Problem
Insurance fraud can increase claim costs, reduce profitability, and take resources away from genuine claims. When suspicious claims are not identified early, insurance companies may face unnecessary financial losses and spend more time investigating potentially fraudulent activities. The goal of this analysis is to identify patterns in insurance claims that may help an insurer better understand where fraud risk is higher and where further investigation may be needed.

## Business Questions
The analysis was designed to answer the following questions:
What is the overall fraud rate in the claims data?

Which incident types have the highest fraud rates?

Which incident severity levels are most associated with fraud?

Which vehicle makes have the highest claim costs?

Which locations have the highest fraud exposure?

Which age groups show higher fraud risk?

Are there specific hours of the day when fraud activity is more concentrated?

## Key Insights
**24.7% Fraud Rate:**
Approximately 247 out of every 1,000 claims were flagged as potentially fraudulent.

**Major Damage Shows Higher Fraud Risk:**
Major damage incidents recorded the highest fraud rate among the severity categories analysed.

**Single-Vehicle Collisions Lead Incident Types:**
Single-vehicle collisions recorded the highest fraud rate among the incident types.

**Higher-Risk Age Group Identified:**
Customers aged 60+ represented the highest-risk demographic group in the analysis.

**Fraud Exposure Varies by Location:**
Some cities showed noticeably higher fraud exposure than others, highlighting areas that may require closer attention.

**Fraud Activity Varies by Time:**
Fraud activity was more concentrated during certain hours of the day, providing another dimension for understanding potential risk.

**Vehicle Claim Costs:**
Dodge and Suburu recorded the highest claim costs, making them the leading vehicle make total claim cost.

## Recommendations
**Review High-Risk Claims:**
Claims with several fraud risk factors should be checked more carefully before approval.

**Focus on High-Risk Areas:**
Fraud teams can pay closer attention to incident types, locations, age groups, and times with higher fraud rates.

**Monitor Claims Regularly:**
Regularly reviewing claims data can help identify new fraud patterns early.
